Legal & Compliance
Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: January 15, 2026

1. Commitment to Your Privacy

At TaxCore, protecting your confidential personal and financial data is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect when you access our tax preparation, bookkeeping, and accounting platforms, how we handle and protect that information, and your legal privacy rights under applicable data protection laws.

2. Information We Collect

To provide accurate tax returns, advisory, and financial compliance services, we collect information you provide directly to us:

  • Identity & Contact Details: Full name, email address, physical mailing address, phone number, and account credentials.
  • Government Tax Identifiers: Social Security Number (SSN), Permanent Account Number (PAN), Employer Identification Number (EIN), or GST Identification Number (GSTIN) as mandated by fiscal authorities.
  • Financial Records: W-2 forms, 1099 statements, bank balances, balance sheets, expense receipts, payroll records, and prior years' tax filings.
  • Payment Information: Billing addresses, payment card details processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateways.

3. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected information strictly for legitimate operational and statutory purposes, including:

  • Preparing, reviewing, and electronically filing tax returns and regulatory reports on your behalf.
  • Providing automated tax calendar alerts, filing reminders, and status updates.
  • Securing your TaxCore client dashboard and preventing unauthorized account access.
  • Communicating with you regarding document requirements, accountant queries, and legislative changes.

4. Security & Encryption Standards

TaxCore employs industry-leading security architectures. All sensitive documents and tax identifiers stored in our cloud vaults are protected by 256-bit AES encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit. Access to client documents is strictly restricted to certified accountants and compliance officers handling your specific engagement.

5. Third-Party Sharing & Statutory Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal or financial data under any circumstances. We disclose your data solely to authorized government revenue authorities (such as the IRS or GST Network) for the purpose of executing your filings, or when compelled by lawful legal process.

6. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate records, request deletion of non-essential records (subject to statutory tax record retention requirements), and export copies of your filed returns at any time through your client dashboard.

7. Contact Our Privacy Office

If you have questions, feedback, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our security protocols, please reach out to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@taxcore.com or via our Contact Page.
