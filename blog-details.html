10 Essential Tax Deductions Every Small Business Owner Should Know

Sarah Jenkins

Oct 12, 2026 • 6 min read

Discover common tax deductions that small business owners often overlook and learn how to properly document eligible expenses.

Running a small business requires you to wear many hats. Between managing operations, dealing with clients, and overseeing your team, taxes often take a back seat until the deadline looms. However, understanding your tax deductions is crucial for maximizing your profitability and ensuring your hard-earned money stays within your business.

1. Home Office Deduction

If you use a part of your home exclusively and regularly for conducting business, you may be able to deduct expenses such as mortgage interest, insurance, utilities, repairs, and depreciation. The IRS offers two methods for claiming this deduction:

  • Simplified Option: A standard deduction of $5 per square foot of home used for business (up to 300 square feet).
  • Regular Method: Determining the actual expenses of your home office based on the percentage of your home used for business.
Important Tip

To qualify for the home office deduction, the space must be used exclusively for business. A corner of your dining room table where you occasionally eat dinner does not qualify.

2. Vehicle Use

If you use your car for business, you can deduct the costs. Like the home office deduction, there are two ways to calculate this:

  1. Standard Mileage Rate: A specific rate multiplied by the business miles driven.
  2. Actual Expenses: The business portion of actual expenses (gas, oil, repairs, insurance, depreciation).

Remember to keep a detailed log of your business miles, including the date, destination, purpose, and total miles driven.

Conclusion

Tax preparation doesn't have to be a nightmare if you keep organized records throughout the year. By taking advantage of these deductions, you can significantly lower your taxable income.
