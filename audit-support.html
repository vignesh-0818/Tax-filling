Professional Audit Representation & Defense

Facing a tax audit can be stressful. Our certified professionals provide complete representation and support, handling all communication and document preparation.

From answering IRS or state department inquiries to attending hearings on your behalf, we protect your rights and fight for penalty abatements and favorable resolutions.

Key Benefits

Certified Representation

Licensed CPAs and Enrolled Agents handle all agency interactions.

Penalty Abatement

We identify grounds to minimize or eliminate unwarranted penalties.

Audit Trail Preparation

We organize receipts, invoices, and ledgers into an airtight dossier.

Direct Correspondence

You never have to speak to the tax authorities alone.

How the Process Works

1. Notice Assessment

We review your official audit notice or examination letter to evaluate scope and risks.

2. Evidence Gathering

Our CPAs reconstruct necessary records, logs, and receipts to substantiate filed positions.

3. Defense & Representation

We represent you in all discussions, conferences, and written responses with the tax auditor.

4. Final Resolution & Relief

We secure the closing agreement and apply for penalty reductions wherever applicable.
